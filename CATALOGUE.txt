Subject: Lean 4 formalization of theorem 43 — the classical isoperimetric theorem

Dear Freek,

A Lean 4 formalization of the full classical planar isoperimetric theorem is
available at https://github.com/kim-em/isoperimetric.

For every continuous rectifiable simple closed planar curve, with geometric
length L and actual Lebesgue area A of its bounded Jordan interior, it proves

    4 * pi * A <= L^2,

with equality if and only if the curve's image is a positive-radius circle.
The theorem covers nonconvex curves, either orientation, and arbitrary
continuous rectifiable parametrizations. It requires no smoothness or assumed
area/Fourier identities. This matches the rectifiable simple closed curve scope
of the HOL Light result listed for entry 43 and also proves the circle converse.
